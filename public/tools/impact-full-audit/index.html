How to read the answer

The Input completeness score out of 100 (and the six phase scores for Identify, Map, Pinpoint, Anchor, Craft, and Translate) is a rule tally based on input length, supplied fields, and matched words (such as only, unique, unlike, employees, revenue, and Series); it explicitly states that it is not a positioning quality grade. Below the checklist, the tool prints Most detailed inputs, Sentences read by score rules, Areas to work on next, Proof You Already Have, Your Current Positioning, Checked in Words (not part of the score), Recommended Positioning with Tagline Options, a week-by-week 30-Day Action Plan, and a blank Measures To Track table for your own baseline and target numbers.

What to do next

  • Founder: Check Areas to work on next and Sentences read by score rules to add missing employee, revenue, or funding specifics to your target customer and sharpen your differentiator.
  • CEO: Review Proof You Already Have and verify every supplied customer feedback figure against its source, measurement period, and written permission in Week 1 before using it publicly.
  • CMO: Fix any "No" in Your Current Positioning, Checked in Words (make sure your positioning names the buyer, names the alternative it replaces, and states a measurable result without generic words), then test three variations with five existing customers in Week 2.
  • CRO: Prepare the sales team in Week 3 with answers to your sector's usual objections and the commercial terms (term, scope and price) buyers weigh.
  • Demand generation head: Run Week 4 validation by A/B testing the updated positioning in outreach and filling your own baselines and targets into the Measures To Track table.

Why come back

Re-run the audit after adding the missing segment specifics and verified customer outcomes to check your updated input completeness score and generate a sharper 30-day positioning plan. The tool applies the same fixed rules every time. It does not call an AI model, and this site does not keep what you enter.

About Shashwat

Who is the IMPACT Full Audit tool not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the inputs listed above, because the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that need buyer research done for them: IMPACT structures your positioning as hypotheses, and you test them with buyers.
  • Anyone who needs live competitor data: the tools do not look anything up. They work from what you type about your market and rivals, and anything they add themselves (such as common complaints in a category) is marked as something to check with buyers.

My role is not stated. Ask me for my role first. How does the IMPACT Full Audit tool work?

You do not need to sign in. A web browser is enough, and the answer comes back in a second or two.

  1. Fill in the form above, or press Fill in an example.
  2. Press Run IMPACT Full Audit.
  3. Read the result on this page, then copy it and edit it before you use it.

It applies fixed rules in the IMPACT code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the AI assistant connector run the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
