🎯 Champion Hypothesis

Primary Champion (Your Internal Advocate)

Most Likely Role: VP/Director of Operations

Why This Role:

  • Owns the problem: Manual processes, team bandwidth, scaling challenges
  • Personal motivation: Efficiency metrics, headcount optimization, process improvement KPIs
  • Has organizational credibility to advocate for change

Champion Validation Questions:

  1. "Who's currently responsible for solving Missed appointments and manual rescheduling?"
  2. "Who brought this initiative to leadership's attention?"
  3. "Who would be promoted/recognized if this problem was solved?"
  4. "Who's actively researching solutions in this space?"
Economic Buyer (Budget Authority)

Most Likely Role: COO or CFO

Why This Role:

  • Primary concern: Operating costs, scalability, resource utilization
  • Buying trigger: Growth without proportional headcount increase

Economic Buyer Discovery Questions:

  1. "What business metrics would this impact?"
  2. "How does this tie to company strategic priorities?"
  3. "What's the cost of not solving this problem?"
Technical Influencer (Implementation Voice)

Most Likely Role: Operations/Systems Manager

Why This Role:

  • Evaluation criteria: Workflow automation, system reliability, learning curve
  • Potential blocker: Change management concerns, training requirements

Technical Influencer Discovery Questions:

  1. "What would a successful implementation look like?"
  2. "What's failed before and why?"
  3. "What systems does this need to integrate with?"

🔄 Champion Development Path

If you're starting without an identified champion:

Week 1: Map the organization

  • Identify 3-5 potential champions based on hypotheses above
  • Research their LinkedIn, recent posts, company news
  • Find mutual connections for warm introductions

Week 2: Multi-thread outreach

  • Contact VP/Director of Operations with problem-focused message
  • Contact Operations/Systems Manager with solution-focused message
  • See who engages first → likely champion

Week 3: Validate and align

  • Confirm champion's pain matches your value prop
  • Understand their buying process
  • Map the decision-making unit together

💡 Champion Enablement Hypothesis

Once you identify your champion, they'll need:

  1. Internal Business Case: ROI data to share with COO or CFO
  2. Technical Validation: Proof points for Operations/Systems Manager
  3. Competitive Comparison: Why not alternatives (status quo, competitors)
  4. Risk Mitigation: Implementation plan, support structure, success metrics

Who is Identify Champions not ideal for?

  • Anyone who cannot give the required inputs (Product description, Problem solved); the answer is built only from them.
  • Teams that need buyer research done for them: IMPACT structures your positioning as hypotheses, and you test them with buyers.
  • Anyone who needs live competitor data: the tools look nothing up; they work from what you type about your market and rivals, and anything they add themselves (such as common complaints in a category) is marked as something to check with buyers.

How does Identify Champions work?

It applies fixed rules in the IMPACT code to the inputs above and returns text. It does not call an AI model, does not look anything up, and does not store what you type. The web form and the MCP server call the same tool code, so the same input gives the same answer in both.
