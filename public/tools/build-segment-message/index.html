See the reasoning behind the message

  1. Proof gaps and status quo questions come first.
  2. Identify Champions and Map Alternatives run side by side.
  3. You confirm the champion, target customer wording and alternative.
  4. Pinpoint Value gets that target wording unchanged.
  5. Craft Message gets the chosen alternative, outcome and capability.
  6. Read trigger, measure and mechanism together, then test with buyers.
What if we already agreed a claim in the workshop?

Give it to chat with the brief. The chain starts from that claim and keeps the proof gate in place. A workshop decision does not turn an illustrative result into customer evidence.

What if we have no customer result?

The buyer lines say “unproved hypothesis”. Figures are held out and the next action is to draft discovery questions about the current approach. Company-wide counts do not establish a customer outcome.

Does the tool confirm our champion or research rivals?

It works from your supplied notes. A role title is a hypothesis; confirm advocacy and access to the economic buyer. Matching words about an alternative do not prove its weakness is answered.

When is the message ready?

Keep it a first draft until buyers can repeat it in their own words and say why it matters. The tool gives you five buyer-test questions. For a second pair of eyes, book 45 minutes.

Worked example: CloudSEK (illustrative inputs)

About this example. CloudSEK is a real company. It has not reviewed this page, and Helix GTM Consulting has no relationship with it. CloudSEK describes itself as “a Predictive Cyber Threat Intelligence firm specializing in AI-powered attack detection” and has announced a $10 million Series B2 round (company-reported; CloudSEK announcement, checked 19 November 2026). The product and capability lines below are Helix’s exercise wording, not CloudSEK’s claims. Every input below is illustrative, written by Helix to show how the tool works. None of it is CloudSEK's data, and the output is an exercise result, not CloudSEK's strategy.

We ran Segment Message Builder on 19 November 2026 through the IMPACT MCP endpoint (server version 2.2.28); the excerpt is cut word for word from its answer.

Show the inputs
  • Stage: map
  • Brief: product: CloudSEK, product description: Predictive threat intelligence for leaked credentials and exposed assets, segments: US credit unions with $1B to $10B in assets, problem: we cannot show the examiner who acted on each leaked credential, or when, category: external threat intelligence, alternatives: in-house manual dark-web monitoring; a larger US threat intelligence vendor; doing nothing until a breach, weak points: alerts arrive without an asset owner (our note to test, not a competitor fact), desired outcome: close each leaked-credential finding with an owner and a date, capability: maps each leaked credential to an asset owner with a dated response record, trigger: the next regulatory exam, measure: days from a leaked-credential finding to a recorded owner action, sector: cybersecurity, proof: kind: none

Exercise output from illustrative inputs. It is not CloudSEK's data, result or strategy.

Proof gaps

  • Proof you have: none given for the result yet.
  • Supply a real customer result for this outcome.
  • Supply the source of the customer result.
  • Supply the period actually measured.

Questions about the current approach

  • Who keeps the current approach running today, and what happens when they are away?
  • What breaks in the current approach when your volumes or your plans change?
  • What makes doing nothing acceptable for US credit unions with $1B to $10B in assets today?
  • What would make this problem important enough to change the current approach?
