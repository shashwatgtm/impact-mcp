What if we already agreed a claim in the workshop?

Give it to chat with the brief. The chain starts from that claim and keeps the proof gate in place. A workshop decision does not turn an illustrative result into customer evidence.

What if we have no customer result?

The buyer lines say “unproved hypothesis”. Figures are held out and the next action is to draft discovery questions about the current approach. Company-wide counts do not establish a customer outcome.

Does the tool confirm our champion or research rivals?

It works from your supplied notes. A role title is a hypothesis; confirm advocacy and access to the economic buyer. Matching words about an alternative do not prove its weakness is answered.

When is the message ready?

Keep it a first draft until buyers can repeat it in their own words and say why it matters. The tool gives you five buyer-test questions. For a second pair of eyes, book 45 minutes.
