What is IMPACT? IMPACT is a free web app and AI connector with eight positioning tools built on Shashwat Ghosh's IMPACT framework: Identify champions, Map alternatives, Pinpoint value, Anchor market, Craft message, Translate execution. Describe your product, buyers and rivals, and each tool returns a structured positioning draft with hypotheses to test, or a full audit.

Who is IMPACT not ideal for?

  • Teams that need buyer research done for them: IMPACT structures your positioning as hypotheses, and you test them with buyers.
  • Anyone who needs live competitor data: the tools look nothing up; they work from what you type about your market and rivals, and anything they add themselves (such as common complaints in a category) is marked as something to check with buyers.
  • Teams that need saved history: nothing is stored, so keep each draft where you keep your positioning work.

How do I use IMPACT?

  • In your browser: open a tool above and press Fill in an example to see how it works, then run it with your own details.
  • In Claude or ChatGPT: add IMPACT once as a custom connector, with no sign-in, then ask in plain words. Use it in Claude or ChatGPT has the steps.

Questions and answers

What is IMPACT?

IMPACT is a free web app and AI connector with eight positioning tools built on Shashwat Ghosh's IMPACT framework: Identify champions, Map alternatives, Pinpoint value, Anchor market, Craft message, Translate execution. Describe your product, buyers and rivals, and each tool returns a structured positioning draft with hypotheses to test, or a full audit.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the IMPACT code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. Nothing is stored and nothing you type is logged. The privacy page has the details.
