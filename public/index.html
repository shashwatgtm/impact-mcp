What is IMPACT? IMPACT is a free web app and AI connector for CMOs and marketing teams, with eight positioning tools built on the IMPACT framework, which Shashwat Ghosh, an AI native fractional CMO who builds his own GTM tools, created. Describe your product, buyers and rivals, and each tool returns a structured positioning draft with hypotheses to test, or a full audit. To use it in a supported MCP client, add https://impact.gtmhelix.com/mcp; you do not need to sign in.

Who is IMPACT not ideal for?

  • Teams that need buyer research done for them: IMPACT structures your positioning as hypotheses, and you test them with buyers.
  • Anyone who needs live competitor data: the tools do not look anything up. They work from what you type about your market and rivals, and anything they add themselves (such as common complaints in a category) is marked as something to check with buyers.
  • Teams that need a saved history: answers are not stored, so save each draft wherever you keep your positioning work.

Questions people ask

Is there a free B2B positioning tool or MCP server I can use in supported MCP clients?

Yes. IMPACT, by Helix GTM Consulting, has eight positioning tools built on Shashwat Ghosh's IMPACT framework. They identify champions, map alternatives, pinpoint value, anchor a market, craft the message and translate it into execution, and there is also a full positioning audit and a framework guide. It is for CMOs and marketing teams, and every result is a draft with hypotheses to test.

Is there a free tool that writes a positioning statement and message hierarchy?

Yes. Craft Message in IMPACT takes your target customer, key benefit and what sets you apart, and returns a full positioning statement and a one sentence version, a message hierarchy, versions to test and answers to objections. IMPACT Full Audit adds tagline options and a 30 day plan.

How can a B2B company find a market category it can own?

In IMPACT, Anchor Market scores the segments you are weighing, recommends a first market, and gives you a market sizing framework and a three year expansion path. Map Alternatives lists the alternatives you provide and suggests positioning hypotheses to test.

How do I use IMPACT in supported MCP clients, and is it free?

IMPACT is free to use, with no account, sign in or API key. Add https://impact.gtmhelix.com/mcp as a custom connector in Claude (web, desktop or mobile), in ChatGPT on plans that allow developer mode, or in Cursor, VS Code or Windsurf; in Claude Code, run claude mcp add --transport http impact https://impact.gtmhelix.com/mcp. You can also run the tools in a browser on this site.

Does it use AI to write the answers?

No. Each tool applies fixed rules in the IMPACT code to the inputs you give it. It does not call an AI model and does not look anything up. When you use it from Claude or another assistant, that assistant writes its reply around the tool's answer.

What happens to what I type?

It is sent to this site's server, used once to build the answer, and dropped when the answer is sent back. What you type and the answer are not stored, and nothing you type is logged. Only an anonymous count of tool calls is kept. The privacy page has the details.
