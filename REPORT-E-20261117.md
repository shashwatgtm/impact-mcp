# Job E • IMPACT market sizing by segment

17 November 2026. Repository `shashwatgtm/impact-mcp`. Branch `codex/tam-segment-fix-20261117`. Base `471e2c74b55f804a129763d6001d54bb9453d481`. Code and tests verified here through `64940f8a4b8403429240f6270e1657d8f04db607`.

## Buyer situation and before

A buyer supplied 1,400 companies split equally between two segments, USD 36,000 annual contract value, 40% ICP fit and 5% Year 1 share. The connected `impact_anchor_market` tool attached the entire count to the first segment. It reported USD 50,400,000 TAM, USD 20,160,000 SAM and USD 1,008,000 SOM for that segment.

## Why it matters to a buyer

The first segment appeared twice as large as the figures support. A buyer could use the aggregate revenue opportunity to justify entering just one segment.

## After

Each segment receives 700 companies, USD 25,200,000 TAM, USD 10,080,000 SAM and USD 504,000 SOM. The combined row has 1,400 companies, USD 50,400,000 TAM, USD 20,160,000 SAM and USD 1,008,000 SOM. Ranking, the recommended segment calculation and the table use the same allocation. Explicit named counts take priority over partial name matches. Missing allocations stay unknown, and an indivisible equal split prompts for whole company counts. Totals add the displayed cents in each column.
